Method, caveats & sources

Basis

Snapshot as at 29 July 2026. Fundamentals — EBITDA, net debt, share counts — are from the most recent filing for each name; the date sits in the Net debt column and on each company card. Forward figures are sell-side consensus, or company guidance where flagged. Prices refresh live; fundamentals do not, and should be re-cut after each results season.
Market cap = live price × shares outstanding, ex-treasury where disclosed. EV = market cap + last reported net financial debt. Forward multiples apply current EV to forward EBITDA, the market convention, so they do not credit forecast deleveraging. Hybrids, minorities and US tax-equity balances are not added to EV; where material the row carries a ⚑ and the company card gives the amount. Median and mean run across the selected names only.

Known comparability issues

Several names book asset-rotation gains inside EBITDA — Acciona Energía (€614m of FY25 EBITDA), Solaria (€37m one-off in LTM), Energiekontor (development disposals), and Grenergy, whose LTM is conversely depressed because no rotation closed in Q1-26. The "Adjust LTM for one-offs" switch removes what can be quantified. Scatec consolidates partly-owned emerging-market SPVs, so its consolidated multiple is not like-for-like. Enel consolidates 70.1% of Endesa — holding both double-counts. EDPR's net debt is ~92% intercompany parent funding and excludes ~€2.9bn of tax equity.

Take-private multiples

The headline deal multiple is EV / last reported full-year EBITDA before closing, as compiled by Finexsi (MergerMarket / Capital IQ) in the AMF-appointed expert report on the Neoen offer — one consistent methodology across six of the deals. Albioma and Neoen are computed here on the same principle and flagged as such. Enterprise values are inclusive of non-recourse project debt in every case. Where the parties never published an EV — Solarpack, Opdenergy, Greenvolt, Albioma, Neoen — it is built from offer equity value plus last reported net debt, and the row says so. Hover any row for the alternative bases; they differ by several turns.
